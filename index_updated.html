A little letter for you

Dear Pondati 🥰,

I love you sooooooo much, Chellakutty! 🫂😘😘😘

You've always been with me through all my ups and downs throughout our first year together. And I know that you'll always be by my side throughout my entire life. 🫂❤️

Na unna epdi love panna aarambichen, ethukaga unna ivlo love panren nu enakke theriyala. 😅 But one thing I know for sure is that naan unna romba true-ah, unmaiya love panren. 🫂😍😘

I can strongly say one thing: "Enna vida yaaralayum unna happy-ah paathukka mudiyathu." ❤️

Even though nammakulla neraya sandaigal vandhaalum, unmela irukkura ennoda love koraiyave koraiyaathu. In fact, ovvoru sandaikku appuramum unmela irukkura love innum adhigamaagitte thaan pogum. ❤️

I love every single moment that I spend with you. 🫣😘 Whether we're laughing together, fighting with each other, teasing each other or just spending time together, every moment is special to me.

You're always my dearest sweetheart! ❤️😘

Something that I have always wished for in my life has finally come true in the form of YOU. ✨

Ennoda life-la enakku yaaru illanalum paravala, aana eppavum nee mattum ennoda kooda oru support-ah irukkanum. That's all I need. 🥺❤️

Bigil-la solluvaangala, "Ella aanoda vettrikku pinnadiyum oru ponnu iruppa" nu. Andha maathiri ennoda ovvoru success-kum nee en kooda irukkanum. Adhe maathiri, unnodaya ovvoru success-kum naan un kooda iruppen. Un happiness, un dreams, un goals ellathulayum naan unakku support-ah iruppen. 🫂❤️

Yaaru namakku support panraangalo illaiyo, namma rendu perum orutharukku oruthar support-ah irukkanum, thangoo! ❤️

I pray that our love never ends and that we stay together through every phase of our life. Namma ippo irukkura indha love, care, understanding ellame namma life full-ah continue aaganum. 🫂😘

I don't know what the future holds for us, but one thing I know for sure is that I want YOU to be a part of every chapter of my life. ❤️

Once again, I LOVE YOU SOOOOO MUCH, CHELLAMEY! 💋💋💋💋💋

With lots and lots of love,
Unnodaya Mama 🥰❤️
